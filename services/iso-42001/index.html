What ISO/IEC 42001 Covers

THE CORE OF
AN AIMS

ISO/IEC 42001 gives organizations a structured, auditable framework for developing, deploying, and monitoring AI responsibly, modeled on the same management-system approach as ISO 27001 and ISO 9001, but purpose-built for AI risk.

01 / REQUIREMENT

AI Risk Assessment & Treatment

Identify, evaluate, and treat risks specific to AI systems, bias, safety, reliability, and unintended impact, with a documented, repeatable methodology.

02 / REQUIREMENT

AI System Lifecycle Controls

Govern AI systems from design and development through deployment, monitoring, and retirement, with clear ownership at every stage.

03 / REQUIREMENT

Data Governance & Quality

Establish controls over the data used to train, fine-tune, and operate AI systems, including provenance, quality, and privacy considerations.

04 / REQUIREMENT

Transparency & Explainability

Document how AI systems make decisions and communicate their capabilities and limitations to affected stakeholders and users.

05 / REQUIREMENT

Third-Party & Supplier AI Management

Extend governance to vendor and third-party AI tools and models your organization relies on but doesn't directly build.

06 / REQUIREMENT

Continuous Monitoring & Improvement

Operate internal audits, management reviews, and corrective action processes that keep the AIMS current as your AI use evolves.

FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

What exactly is ISO/IEC 42001?

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the world's first international standard for an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). It gives organizations a structured framework for governing how AI systems are designed, developed, deployed, and monitored, covering risk management, data governance, transparency, and continuous improvement.

Who needs this certification?

Any organization that develops, deploys, or relies heavily on AI systems, whether building AI products, using AI internally, or procuring AI tools from vendors, benefits from an AIMS. It's increasingly requested in enterprise and government procurement processes.

Does Cloud Guardian issue the certificate?

No. Certification is issued by an independent, accredited certification body after a formal audit. Cloud Guardian prepares your organization, building the management system, documentation, and internal audit, and supports you through that external audit process.

How long does certification typically take?

Most organizations move from gap assessment to certification audit in 4-9 months, depending on organization size, AI system complexity, and how much governance infrastructure already exists.

How is ISO/IEC 42001 different from ISO/IEC 27001?

ISO 27001 governs information security management. ISO/IEC 42001 governs the management of AI systems specifically, risks like bias, explainability, and AI-specific lifecycle controls that fall outside a traditional security management system. Many organizations pursue both, and existing ISO 27001 programs make 42001 easier to layer in.
