Migration Paths

ANY PLATFORM,
ANY DIRECTION

Every migration includes mail, calendars, contacts, groups, aliases, and shared mailboxes, plus DNS, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC re-pointing so deliverability never breaks.

01 / PATH

Google Workspace → Office 365

Full Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Groups migration into Exchange Online, Teams, and SharePoint, licensing guidance included.

02 / PATH

Office 365 → Google Workspace

Exchange Online, OneDrive, and SharePoint moved cleanly into Gmail and Drive when Google is the better fit for your team.

03 / PATH

On-Prem Exchange → Cloud

Retire aging Exchange servers. Hybrid or cutover migrations to Exchange Online or Google Workspace, decommissioning included.

04 / PATH

IMAP / POP & Legacy Hosts

GoDaddy, cPanel, Rackspace, and other legacy mail hosts migrated to a modern platform with your history intact.

05 / PATH

Tenant-to-Tenant

Mergers, acquisitions, and rebrands: consolidate Microsoft 365 or Google tenants, domains, and identities without losing a thread.

06 / PATH

File & Data Migrations

Drive ↔ SharePoint/OneDrive, file servers to the cloud, and permissions mapped so people keep access to what they had.
