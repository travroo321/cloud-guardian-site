What This Covers

TWO HALVES OF
THE SAME JOB

Being found and being run well are not separate problems. Your site, your domain, your mail, your CRM and your phones are one system, and the reason a business is invisible online is usually the same reason its invoices go out late.

01 / HALF

Digital presence, so you get found

The website, the Google listing, the reviews, the local search footprint, and increasingly whether ChatGPT and the other assistants mention you when somebody asks for what you sell. Built and then actually maintained.

02 / HALF

vCIO, so you get run

Vendor consolidation, a technology budget, a roadmap tied to what the business is trying to do, and someone accountable in the room when a decision has to be made.

Questions

STRAIGHT
ANSWERS

Is this the same as hiring a marketing agency?

No, and the difference is access. An agency can tell you what to publish. It cannot fix the DNS record that is putting your email in spam, consolidate the four tools doing one job, or change how your CRM writes data. We can, because we already hold the keys to the systems the advice depends on.

Do we have to move our IT to you as well?

No. This is sold on its own and plenty of clients keep their existing IT arrangement. It works better together, because the same team seeing the tickets sees where the friction is, but it is not a condition.

What size business is this for?

Roughly ten to two hundred people. Below that the problem is usually simpler than this. Above that you generally want a full-time person, and we will say so rather than sell you a retainer you have outgrown.

What does it cost?

It is quoted per engagement rather than per user, because a twelve person firm with one clean system and a forty person firm with nine of them are not the same job. The mapping exercise is what produces the number, and that starts with the free assessment.
