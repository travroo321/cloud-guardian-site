Pricing FAQ

THE QUESTIONS
BEHIND THE NUMBER

How can you be half the price of my current provider?

Because it is a different cost structure, not a discount. A legacy MSP carries a tiered help desk where a ticket passes through two people who cannot fix it before reaching one who can, on tooling bought a decade ago. We automate the volume work and use AI to triage, correlate and close the noise, so we carry far less labour per managed endpoint. That difference goes into the price rather than into a margin. During the assessment we show you the line items so you can check it against your current invoice.

Is there a minimum number of users?

No. Most providers set a floor at 10 or 20 seats because small accounts are unprofitable under a staffed model. Ours is not, so there is no floor.

What is the contract term?

Month to month on Essential. Managed plans are typically annual because the onboarding work is front-loaded, but there is no three-year lock and no exit fee. If you leave, your documentation goes with you, because it was always yours.

What is not included?

Third-party licensing unless you buy it through us, hardware, project work such as cabling and wireless surveys, and after-hours work outside the agreed coverage on Essential. All of it is quoted before it happens. You will not find a line on an invoice you have not already seen.

Why is incident response $90 an hour on Essential but included above it?

Because Essential is deliberately a floor, not a full service. You are paying for the controls that stop most incidents rather than for someone standing by. On Managed and above, response is included, because at that point preventing the incident and fixing it are the same job and we should not be paid more when we do the first part badly.

Do the prices change if we grow?

Per user, so yes, proportionally. Adding people changes the figure on the next cycle. Nothing else moves without a conversation first.

What does the free assessment actually involve?

A full inventory of devices, accounts, licensing and exposure, an external check for what is already reachable from the internet, and a search for your credentials in breach data. You get written findings with prioritised remediation. If you do not hire us, you keep the report and can hand it to whoever you do hire.
