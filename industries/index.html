FAQ

INDUSTRY
QUESTIONS

What if my industry is not listed?

Call us. These ten are where we have the deepest pattern knowledge, but the underlying work of managed IT, security, networking, cabling, and phones applies everywhere. If we are not the right fit for your sector we will say so directly rather than learning on your budget.

How is an industry page different from a service page?

Service pages describe what we do: managed IT, cybersecurity audits, cabling, VoIP, migrations. Industry pages describe the environment we do it in, including the platforms you run, the regulation you answer to, and the specific ways IT fails in your sector. Most buyers need both, in that order.

Do you really cost half of what our current provider charges?

For comparable coverage, that is the range clients report when they switch. The reason is structural rather than promotional: our delivery is automated and AI-assisted, so we carry far less labor per managed endpoint than a provider running a tiered help desk on tooling from a decade ago. We will show you the line items during the assessment so you can verify it against your current invoice.

Do you handle compliance certification?

We implement and evidence technical controls, and we produce documentation auditors and examiners can use. For ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems we take organizations through gap assessment, implementation, and certification audit support. We are not a law firm or an accreditation body, and any IT provider claiming to certify your HIPAA or PCI compliance is misrepresenting how those regimes work.

Can you support more than one of these at once?

Yes, and many clients sit in two. A dental group is healthcare and multi-location retail operations. A construction firm doing municipal work is construction and public sector. We build one engagement that accounts for both rather than making you choose a label.
