Accounting and Finance · Reality Check

THE FOUR THINGS
FIRMS GET WRONG

From assessments of accounting and advisory practices across Central New Jersey.

01 📄

A WISP that exists as a PDF and nothing else

Downloaded from a template, dated two years ago, naming a security coordinator who has left. It describes controls that were never configured. Under the FTC Safeguards Rule, a plan you are not executing is worse than no plan, because it documents exactly what you failed to do.

02 📧

Client documents moving by plain email

W-2s, K-1s, full Social Security numbers, and bank statements arriving as unencrypted attachments, then living in a mailbox forever. Business email compromise in this sector usually starts by watching that mailbox and then sending a very convincing wire instruction.

03 🐌

Tax software that dies in March

Hosted desktops sized for October, network bandwidth shared with everything else, and a server that was adequate two seasons ago. Nobody notices until the week the returns have to go out and every screen takes eight seconds to redraw.

04 🗄️

Seven years of records with no retention policy

Client data kept indefinitely because deleting felt risky. Every additional year retained is additional breach scope. Retention is a security control, not just a filing habit.

Accounting and Finance FAQ

QUESTIONS WE GET
FROM ACCOUNTING AND FINANCE CLIENTS

Do you write the WISP or do we?

We write the technical sections and implement every control they describe, then hand you the document and the evidence. The administrative and training portions need firm ownership because they describe what your people do, and we will draft them with you rather than for you.

Which tax and accounting platforms do you work with?

UltraTax, Lacerte, ProSeries, Drake, CCH Axcess, ProSystem fx, TaxAct Professional, QuickBooks Desktop and Online, Sage, Xero, and hosted-desktop environments from Rightworks and similar providers. We manage the environment those run in and the performance of it during season.

Can you get us off email attachments for client documents?

Yes, and it is usually the highest-value single change we make in this sector. A secure client portal with authenticated upload, expiring links, and a record of who accessed what, integrated into how your staff already works so it actually gets used.

What does this cost compared with our current provider?

Our Small Business plan starts at $20 per user per month for baseline security coverage with incident response at $90 per hour, and fully managed IT is quoted after a free assessment. Firms switching to us from a legacy MSP typically land near half their prior spend for broader coverage, because our delivery is automated rather than staffed with escalation layers.

Can you handle a peer review or a client due diligence request?

Yes. We produce the technical documentation those requests ask for, including control descriptions, configuration evidence, and backup verification records.
