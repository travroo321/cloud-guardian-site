Schools and Education · Reality Check

WHAT BREAKS
DURING A SCHOOL DAY

The recurring pattern in school assessments.

01 📶

Wi-Fi that fails at 8:15 and during testing

Access points placed for coverage instead of capacity, so a classroom of thirty devices contends for one radio. Standardized testing then becomes an IT emergency every spring. High-density design is a different exercise from coverage design and cannot be retrofitted with more access points.

02 💻

A device fleet nobody controls

Chromebooks and iPads handed out without enrollment, so patching, filtering, app control, and recovery all depend on the student's cooperation. At year end a meaningful share of the fleet simply does not come back.

03 📂

Student records shared by convenience

Grades, IEP documents, and behavioral records emailed between staff and stored in personal drives. FERPA expects access control over education records, and a shared spreadsheet in a mailbox is the opposite of that.

04 🎮

Filtering students route around in a week

Bypasses spread faster than any policy update. Filtering has to be enforced at the device and identity level, not only at the network edge, or it stops working the moment a student uses cellular or a VPN app.

Schools and Education FAQ

QUESTIONS WE GET
FROM SCHOOLS AND EDUCATION CLIENTS

Do you work with public districts or only private schools?

Both. Private and charter schools are our most common education clients because they buy directly, but we also respond to public district and municipal solicitations. See our local government and public sector page for how we handle bids and RFPs.

Can you design Wi-Fi for standardized testing loads?

Yes, and that is the load we design to rather than average daily use. We survey to per-room device counts and validate with testing-day conditions in mind, because a network sized for the average will fail on the day it matters most.

Do you manage Chromebook and iPad fleets?

Yes. Google Admin and Apple School Manager enrollment, policy and filtering, app deployment, patching, inventory, and recovery controls. We also handle the summer refresh and re-enrollment cycle.

Are you E-Rate familiar?

We design and document infrastructure with E-Rate categories in mind so eligible work is clearly separated from ineligible work, and we support your filing consultant with the technical documentation and quotes they need. We are not a filing agent.

Can you provide filtering that meets our safety obligations?

Yes. Device and identity level filtering with category policy, reporting, and alerting on concerning search or content activity, applied consistently on and off campus for school-owned devices.
