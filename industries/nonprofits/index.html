Nonprofits · Reality Check

WHERE NONPROFIT IT
GOES WRONG

What we find in nonprofit assessments, repeatedly.

01 💰

Paying commercial rates for licensing

Microsoft offers grants and deep discounts for eligible 501(c)(3) organizations, Google Workspace has a nonprofit tier, and TechSoup brokers heavily discounted software. Many organizations we assess are paying full commercial price on all of it. Claiming what you qualify for often funds the entire IT improvement.

02 👤

Volunteers and board members with permanent access

A board member from four years ago still has a shared drive login. A volunteer who ran one event still receives donor exports. Access accumulates and nobody prunes it, which is the most common finding in this sector.

03 🎁

Donor data spread across everything

Spreadsheets on desktops, exports in email, a CRM nobody trusts, and payment records in a fourth place. A donor data breach is a fundraising problem as much as a security problem, because the damage lands on trust.

04 📞

Nobody actually responsible for IT

A staff member who is good with computers holds it together on top of their real job. It works until they leave, and then nothing is documented and no vendor relationship is in anyone's name.

Nonprofits FAQ

QUESTIONS WE GET
FROM NONPROFITS CLIENTS

Is there a nonprofit rate?

The larger saving is usually not our fee, it is the licensing and cloud spend we recover for you. That said, Silver at $90 an hour with no monthly fee at all, and Gold at $65 per user per month, are both priced well below legacy MSP coverage with no minimums and no long lock-in, which is why they fit nonprofit budgets and board approval cycles.

Can you help us qualify for Microsoft or Google nonprofit programs?

Yes. We handle the eligibility verification and enrollment process, then rebuild your tenant on the licensing you are entitled to rather than what you were sold.

Which nonprofit CRMs do you support?

Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge and eTapestry, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Neon CRM, Little Green Light, and Kindful. We manage the identity, access, integration, and backup side around them.

We have almost no IT budget. Where do we start?

A free assessment. We map what you have, identify licensing and cloud waste, and quantify what you would recover. In this sector the assessment frequently pays for the first year of managed service out of savings alone.

Can you support volunteers who are not staff?

Yes, and it should be structured differently from staff access: time-limited accounts, minimum necessary permissions, no local data storage, and automatic expiry rather than manual cleanup.
