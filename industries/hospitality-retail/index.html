Hospitality and Retail · Reality Check

WHAT COSTS YOU
A SERVICE

The problems that actually shut a floor down.

01 💳

Payment and guest traffic on one network

The card reader, the register, the kitchen display, the security camera with a default password, and the guest Wi-Fi all on the same flat segment. That is both a live security exposure and directly contrary to PCI DSS expectations for isolating the cardholder data environment.

02 📱

Handhelds and tablets that drop mid-order

Dining room and patio coverage designed by mounting one access point near the host stand. Servers lose the order, re-enter it, and the ticket times go up during exactly the busiest hour.

03 🏪

Six locations, six different builds

Every store or restaurant set up by a different vendor at a different time, so every problem is a novel problem and nothing can be fixed remotely. Standardizing the build is the single change that reduces support cost most in this sector.

04 🍳

Kitchen and back-of-house treated as an afterthought

KDS screens, printers, and prep-area wireless in a hot, greasy, high-interference environment, installed with office assumptions. They fail in ways nobody diagnoses until service is already backed up.

Hospitality and Retail FAQ

QUESTIONS WE GET
FROM HOSPITALITY AND RETAIL CLIENTS

Do you work with our POS vendor?

Yes. We own the network, segmentation, connectivity, and cabling that the point of sale runs on, and we coordinate with Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Aloha, Micros, or whichever platform you use on the application side. When there is a dispute about cause, we bring measurements.

Can you segment payments without replacing our equipment?

Usually yes. Segmentation is largely a configuration and switching exercise. Sometimes a switch or firewall genuinely needs replacing, and if so we will tell you which one and why rather than quoting a full refresh.

Will you install without closing us?

Yes. Cabling, cutovers, and network changes are scheduled overnight or between services. Losing a service period costs far more than the off-hours labor.

Can guest Wi-Fi be captive-portal branded?

Yes, including branded splash pages, terms acceptance, session limits, and bandwidth caps so guest traffic cannot degrade operations. It stays on its own segment with no path to payment or back-office systems.

Do you support multiple locations under one agreement?

Yes, and that is where this sector saves the most. One standard build, central management, one phone system, one point of contact, and per-location pricing that does not multiply overhead.
