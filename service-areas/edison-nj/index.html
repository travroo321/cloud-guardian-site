01 / EDISON

Warehouse and Distribution Wireless

Barcode scanners, forklift-mounted terminals, and inventory systems fail in ways office Wi-Fi never does. Metal racking reflects signal, inventory levels change coverage daily, and high ceilings need directional antennas placed deliberately, not access points screwed to a wall.

02 / EDISON

Structured Cabling at Scale

Warehouse and flex-space build-outs need cabling engineered for distance, interference, and expansion. We run Cat6A and fiber, test every run, and hand you documentation so the next contractor is not guessing.

03 / EDISON

Logistics Software Uptime

WMS, TMS, and EDI integrations do not tolerate downtime. When the system that tells your team what to pick goes down, the whole floor stops. Monitoring has to catch problems before they reach the floor.

04 / EDISON

Multi-Location Networking

Edison operations frequently span a warehouse, a separate office, and remote staff. Site to site connectivity, unified phone systems, and consistent security policy across all three is where most providers fall short.

Edison FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

Do you do wireless site surveys for Edison warehouses?

Yes, and warehouse surveys are a distinct engagement from office surveys. We walk the facility with survey software, account for racking and inventory height, analyze 2.4, 5, and 6 GHz bands, and deliver heatmaps with access point placement engineered to hold signal above -67 dBm in every aisle you need coverage.

Can you run cabling in an occupied warehouse?

Yes. We schedule runs around your operating hours, including nights and weekends, so picking and shipping never stop for our install.

Do you support businesses along the Route 1 corridor?

Yes. Edison, Metuchen, Woodbridge, Iselin, and the surrounding Route 1 and Turnpike corridor are all inside our standard onsite service area.

What if our warehouse Wi-Fi already exists but keeps dropping?

That is one of the most common calls we get. We survey what you have, identify whether the cause is coverage gaps, channel interference, access point placement, or capacity, and give you a fix scoped to your building instead of a proposal to replace everything.
