Hillsborough FAQ

QUESTIONS FROM
HILLSBOROUGH BUSINESSES

Do you cover Hillsborough and western Somerset County?

Yes. Hillsborough, Montgomery, Branchburg and the Route 206 corridor are within our onsite service area, with 24/7 remote support covering everything in between.

Our building is large and the Wi-Fi does not reach. Can you fix it?

Yes, and it is usually a design problem rather than an equipment problem. We survey the actual building, account for construction materials and distance, and specify placement and antenna types rather than adding more access points to a bad layout.

Is there a plan for a very small business?

Yes. Silver is $90 an hour, billed in half hour increments, with no monthly fee, no minimum hours and no long-term lock-in.
