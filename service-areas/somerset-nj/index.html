Somerset FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

Do you cover Somerset County?

Yes. Somerset, Franklin Township, Bridgewater, Somerville, Bound Brook, and the surrounding Somerset County area are within our onsite service area.

Can you run fiber between buildings on a campus?

Yes. Inter-building fiber, pathway planning, termination, testing, and full documentation. We handle the coordination with property management that campus work always requires.

Do you have experience with laboratory or research environments?

Yes. Lab environments raise specific issues around instrument connectivity, data integrity, and recovery planning. We treat research data as irreplaceable, because it usually is.

What if our building's landlord controls the network infrastructure?

That is common in corporate parks. We work within landlord-controlled risers and shared infrastructure, handle the approval process, and design around the restrictions rather than fighting them.
