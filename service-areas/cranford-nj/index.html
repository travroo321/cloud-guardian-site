Cranford FAQ

QUESTIONS FROM
CRANFORD BUSINESSES

Should we still have a server in the building?

Often not, and in a flood-prone area that question answers itself faster. We assess what genuinely needs to stay local, move the rest, and make sure the remaining equipment is not sitting somewhere water reaches.

Can you migrate us to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace without downtime?

Yes. Migrations are planned around your calendar with off-hours cutovers, including DNS, SPF, DKIM and DMARC repointing so mail keeps flowing and does not start failing authentication afterward.

Do you cover Cranford and central Union County?

Yes. Cranford, Westfield, Garwood, Clark, Kenilworth and the surrounding area are within our onsite service area.
