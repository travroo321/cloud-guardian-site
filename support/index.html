Self-Help

TRY THESE
FIRST

The steps we walk people through most often. If one of these fixes it, you just saved yourself a ticket.

  • 🔌
    Nothing works at allPower cycle in order and wait between each step: modem, then firewall or router, then switch, then your computer. Give each one a full minute before the next. Out of order is why this often fails to help.
  • 📧
    Email stopped sending or receivingCheck the provider status links above first. Then confirm you are actually online, check whether your mailbox is full, and look in Junk for the missing message before assuming it never arrived.
  • 🖨️
    Printer is offlinePower cycle the printer, confirm it holds the same IP address it had before, and clear the print queue. A printer that changes IP after a power outage is the single most common cause.
  • 🐛
    Everything is slowRestart the machine properly rather than closing the lid. Uptime of several weeks with dozens of browser tabs explains most slowness we get called about.
  • 🎣
    You got a suspicious emailDo not click, do not reply, do not forward it to a colleague. Forward it to us and delete nothing until we have looked. If you already clicked, call (732) 743-5472 now, not tomorrow, because the first hour matters more than the next week.
  • 🔐
    Locked out of your accountDo not attempt more logins, that extends the lockout. Call or text us and we will verify you and reset it. We will never ask you for your password to do it.
  • 📶
    Wi-Fi keeps dropping in one spotNote the exact locations and times and send them with your ticket. A pattern in the data is what turns a vague complaint into a fixable coverage problem.
  • 🆕
    New hire startingGive us the start date, the name, the role, and who they should have the same access as. Five business days notice means their machine and accounts are ready on day one.

Support FAQ

HOW SUPPORT
ACTUALLY WORKS

What are your support hours?

Monitoring and emergency response run 24/7, every day of the year. Standard requests are handled during business hours, and P0 or P1 issues page an on-call engineer regardless of the hour.

How fast will someone respond?

Phoned-in P0 and P1 issues reach a live engineer immediately. Tickets are triaged continuously, with the AI agent resolving common in-scope issues within minutes and everything else routed to an engineer. Response commitments for your specific agreement are written into it rather than implied.

Do I have to use the portal, or can I just call?

Call. We are not the kind of provider that hides behind a ticket system. The portal exists because some people prefer it and because a written record is useful, not because we want a buffer between you and an engineer.

Can you get onsite if remote cannot fix it?

Yes. Middlesex and Somerset County is typically same business day and often same hour. The wider Tri-State area is same or next business day depending on severity.

What if I am not a client yet?

Call anyway. If you are in the middle of an incident we will tell you honestly whether we can help and what it costs. Otherwise start with a free assessment and we will show you what your environment actually looks like.

Is there an authenticated client portal with ticket history?

It is rolling out to managed clients now, covering ticket history, asset inventory, and your documentation. Request access and we will enable your account.
